Figure 2. Simple-compound score against root biomass in the plant-active season (H1a, test 2). Points are samples (100% irrigation, 5 h, water-subtracted per-C values); n = 48 active-season samples on 8 dates. Crosses: root-dense cores flagged by modified z-score |M| > 3.5 (n = 6; SM1080, SM1078, SM1621, SM1126, SM1054, SM1150). Lines are least-squares fits for display, with all cores (dashed) and without flagged cores (solid). Statistics are Spearman rank correlations. x axis is log10. No error bars.
