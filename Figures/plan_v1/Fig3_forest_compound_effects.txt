Figure 3. Season effect for each compound (test 5) and for the group scores (tests 1 and 3). Each compound's water-subtracted per-C value (5 h, 100% irrigation) was z-scored across 77 samples and averaged to 13 date means (8 active, 5 dormant). Points: dormant minus active difference of date means; bars: Welch 95% t-interval. Blue: simple compounds (n = 15); orange: polymers (n = 5); dark grey: glucose (reference). Summary rows: simple-group mean score (test 1), polymer-group mean score, and the paired polymer − simple difference computed per date (test 3). Compounds are sorted by effect within group. No multiple-comparison correction (see cp2_t5_compound_effects.csv for FDR-adjusted p-values).
