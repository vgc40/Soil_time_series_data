Figure S7. Plot-level lag models (plan §5.4). For each gene set (n = 26), linear mixed models with a random intercept for date, fitted by maximum likelihood on plot-matched pairs (34 plot-dates for SIR sets, 35 for enzyme sets; both models on the same rows). Response and predictor z-scored. Points above the dashed 1:1 line have a higher marginal R² for next-date potential than for same-date potential. AIC values are in cp4_lag_models_compare.csv. No error bars.
