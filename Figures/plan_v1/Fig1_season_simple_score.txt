Figure 1. Simple-compound score by season (H1a, test 1). Points are date means of the sample-level simple score (mean z-score of 15 simple compounds; water-subtracted, 5 h, 100% irrigation). n = 13 dates (8 active, 5 dormant), 77 samples. Black points and bars: season mean of date means and its 95% t-interval. Annotation: dormant minus active difference with Welch 95% CI, Welch and Wilcoxon rank-sum p-values. Right panel: the same score after dividing each value by microbial biomass C, shown because its result differs from the per-C result in significance (plan §3.4). Open circles: dates whose mean includes a sample with MBC replaced (0 set to 0.07 µg C/g) or the lowest measured MBC (0.14 µg C/g); see deviation log D1.
