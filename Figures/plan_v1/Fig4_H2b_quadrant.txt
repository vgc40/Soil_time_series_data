Figure 4. Seasonal concordance of expression and substrate-use potential (H2b). One point per compound with an approved gene set (n = 19; 3 extracellular polymer sets, 16 intracellular). Effects are dormant minus active differences of date means of z-scores on the 7 transcript dates (2 dormant, 5 active; 100% irrigation). Expression: centred log ratio of the gene-set sum (35 samples, 5 plots per date). Potential: water-subtracted 5 h SIR (MicroResp samples on the same dates, 6 plots per date). (A) Potential per mg C, the main version since deviation D11. (B) Potential per µg MBC, the plan's original version (robustness a); the effects are nearly identical across compounds because one sample (SM1625, 2024-05-08, MBC replaced with 0.07 µg C/g) dominates every compound's z-scores. Concordant compounds fall in the upper-right and lower-left quadrants. Sign test: two-sided binomial test against 50%. No error bars.
