Figure 5. Lag advantage for each gene set (H2c). For each set, r_same is the Pearson correlation between date-mean expression (centred log ratio) and date-mean potential on the same date, and r_lag between expression at a transcript date and potential at the next sampling date, both over 7 date pairs with plots matched (5 plots per pair; 4 for 2024-01-02 to 2024-02-12 for SIR sets). Points: r_lag − r_same; bars: 95% percentile bootstrap CI from 2,000 resamples of date pairs. Potential: water-subtracted 5 h SIR per mg C for compound sets (D11; n = 16 intracellular, 3 extracellular) and measured activity for enzyme sets (n = 7, extracellular). Vertical coloured lines: group means. Subtitle: permutation test of the difference in means (10,000 label shuffles).
