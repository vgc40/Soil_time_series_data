Figure 6. Example pathway: chitin and N-acetylglucosamine (NAG). Each series is z-scored across its own date means for display. (A) Chitin gene-set expression (chitinases and chitin-binding protein; centred log ratio; 7 transcript dates, 5 plots each), chitin potential (water-subtracted 5 h SIR per mg C, the main version, and per µg MBC for comparison; 13 dates), and NAG enzyme activity (13 dates). (B) NAG uptake and catabolism expression (7 dates) with NAG potential per mg C and per µg MBC (13 dates). 100% irrigation; 5-6 plots per date. The per-MBC series peak on 2024-02-12 and 2024-05-08 because of samples SM1539 and SM1625 (lowest and replaced MBC; deviation log D1). Grey shading: plant-dormant period. No error bars.
