Figure S2. Ratio of the 24 h to the 5 h water-subtracted per-C rate by season and group (test 8). For each date and group, the mean rate over all plots and group compounds at 24 h was divided by the same mean at 5 h (deviation log D6). Whether the 24 h value is the rate over 5-24 h or 0-24 h is not stated in the data package (open item O1). Points: dates (n = 13; 8 active, 5 dormant; 100% irrigation). Black points and bars: season mean and 95% t-interval. Dashed line: ratio of 1.
