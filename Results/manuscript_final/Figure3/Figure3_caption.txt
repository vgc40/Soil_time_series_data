Figure 3. Seasonal field gene expression is structured but does not necessarily track laboratory substrate-response capacity. (A) Bray-Curtis NMDS of BRITE C transcript composition for the
100% irrigation treatment. Field-expressed functional composition differed between plant-active and plant-dormant samples (PERMANOVA R2 = 0.234, p = 0.001; stress = 0.089). Multivariate
dispersion did not differ between seasons (p = 0.92). (B) Temporal expression of BRITE C pathways biologically linked a priori to substrates included in the MicroResp assay. Rows show
pathway-specific z-scores calculated from date-mean transcript expression, so colors represent expression relative to each pathway's own temporal mean rather than absolute expression. Row
annotations indicate the associated biological MicroResp substrate group. (C) Temporal comparison of the three strongest FDR-supported MicroResp responses identified independently in Figure
2 (Methyl oleate, Chitosan, L Glutamine) with expression of their mapped BRITE C functions. MicroResp responses were standardized separately within substrate and transcript expression was
standardized separately within mapped pathway before date-level summarization. Z-scores therefore show relative temporal changes within each measurement and should not be interpreted as
equivalent absolute magnitudes between MicroResp and transcript expression.
